## Launch bay rules (owner, 2026-11-19)
- **Engineering (Ryan) is in charge of the bay** and does the shouting. Above 8 kt with the doors open he says the bay is taking a beating; above 10 kt: "Captain, ya killing the bay doors at this speed! I recommend we slow down." (events `bay-warn`, `bay-door-damage`; spoken in `app.js`). Warnings repeat if the ship slows under 8 kt and then speeds up again with the doors still open.
- **Launching and receiving take 1 minute** with the bay doors open (`LAUNCH_S`). WSKRs, Remora and the shuttle all use the bay.
- **One craft at a time.** A second launch waits ("The bay is busy").
- **Below 8 kt** (`BAY_SAFE_KT`) the bay is safe. **Above 8 kt with the doors open the ship takes damage** at 0.015% hull per knot over 8 per game second (a 60 s launch at 12 kt costs about 3.6%).
- **Above 10 kt with the doors open the doors are damaged permanently** (`doorDmg`, 0.02% per knot over 10 per game second; needs a **dry dock** to repair, none exists yet: `repairDoors()` is the hook). Door damage makes every launch slower (x (1 + damage/50)) and at 100% the doors will not open.
- **Limits are held, not silently broken (owner, 2026-11-22).** The game must not let a limit be exceeded without warning the Captain first, or the Captain overriding it. For the bay: while the **outer doors** are open (an operation, or Remora's tether) the helm **holds the ship at 8 kt** through `sim.speedCaps` and says so once (`speed-cap`: "I am holding us at 8 knots… Tell me to override the limit, or use Systems"). A **launch or recovery above 8 kt is refused** ("Slow below 8 kt first", `why: 'fast'`). The Captain **overrides** with Marcus's order **Override the bay speed limit** (Speed folder, only while the doors are open) or the **Override speed limit** button on the Systems door card (`setBayOverride()`, `bay().override`). The override **lapses when the doors close**. Restore it with **Restore the bay speed limit** or the same button. After an override the damage rules above apply in full. The same pattern (hold, warn, override) is the model for other limits; the Tern depth cap (`sim.depthCaps`, event `depth-cap`) already warns.
- **Systems page, Launch bay doors card (2026-11-22):** two rows. **Outer doors (open to the sea):** CLOSED, or OPEN with the reason (launching, taking aboard, held by Remora's tether); the speed limits, current speed and door damage. **Inner doors (open to the ship):** OPEN normally, CLOSED and sealed while the outer doors are open (interlock; display only, the bay room can still be entered).
- **The doors are open for an operation and for as long as Remora is out** (see below), so the speed rules apply the whole time her tether is out.
- Not allowed while docked (undock first), same as before.
- **WSKR recall is two steps:** the WSKR flies home and waits alongside (`w.arrived`, stays with the ship); when the bay is free, Remora is not out and the ship is at or under 8 kt the bay takes it aboard (1 minute). Above 8 kt the sensors officer says to slow down once; the bay page has **Take aboard now** to force it (the damage rules apply). With Remora out, Engineering says her tether holds the doors open (`bay-tether`). A flat-battery WSKR is also taken aboard once the ship reaches it.
- **Charging:** a racked WSKR charges at 100% per 2 hours (`WSKR_CHARGE_H`), so the time to full is proportional to what was used (a 50% battery takes 1 hour). A WSKR needs at least **20%** to launch. Launched WSKRs take the charge their slot reached. **WSKR battery life is 6 h, 12 h or 24 h depending on research** (`WSKRS.md`, `SCIENCE_LAB.md`); the 2 h recharge does not change. Saved: `navigation.rack` (slot → %), `navigation.bay` (door damage, running operation, `base`, `rearm`, `override`). The WSKR page (Sensors) shows a racked slot's charge and has a Launch command.
- Later automation (relaunch, Launch all, Marcus slowing the ship): `BAY_AUTOMATION.md`.

## Remora in the bay (owner, 2026-11-19)
- Launch and recovery are bay operations like the others (`launchRemora()`, `recoverRemora()`; the buttons on the Remora page and the bay page both use them). `sim.launchRov` and `sim.recoverRov` are still the instant, rule-free calls underneath (tests use them).
- **Her tether keeps the doors open the whole time she is out.** While she is out **no other craft can launch or be taken aboard** ("Remora's tether is holding the bay doors open"), and the speed rules run continuously. Recovery needs her within 0.2 nm.
- **No charging**, but after every recovery **the science team unload the samples and re-equip her**: **5 minutes plus 2 minutes for each sample** collected on that dive (`REMORA_REARM_S`, `REMORA_UNLOAD_S`; `bay().rearm = { t, dur, load }`). She cannot launch until it is done ("remora-ready"). Dr. Patel's side speaks (`remora-aboard`, `remora-ready`). Saved with the bay. (`REMORA.md` is newer: one sample per 2 minutes, each reaches the lab when unloaded.)

## The shuttle: Tern
- Name chosen 2026-11-19 (`SHUTTLE_NAME` in `topics.js`). **Not the Sea Crab**, which belongs to a Sea Quest boat. Ferries **crew and supplies** to and from the Aqua Nova **so it does not have to dock**, and carries **away teams**. (Rescues: `MISSION_GENERATOR.md`. Orders: `TERN_ORDERS.md`.)
- **On the nav map (owner, 2026-11-22):** while she is out of the bay the chart (`nd.js`) shows the Tern as a **pink arrowhead marked Tn**, pointing along her heading (red when adrift). Remora is a violet Rm, WSKRs yellow or orange diamonds. Not drawn on craft views.
- **20 kt. Battery: 1 hour on a full charge to start; research makes it 3 hours, then 6** (`shuttleHours()`; topics **Tern long-life cells** (3 points) and **Tern deep-cycle cells** (5, needs the first)). Draw 1.0 moving, 0.3 at rest; range shown keeps a 15% reserve (about 17 nm to start, 102 nm at 6 h). **Recharges in the bay or at a dock, 2 hours for a full charge, in proportion to the battery used.** Needs 20% to launch. At 15% on the way out it turns back by itself; at 0% it is adrift until the ship comes alongside (0.15 nm). (Not tied to the WSKR research.)
- **Air:** the shuttle carries **air for 8 hours with all 8 seats taken** (2 pilots and 6 passengers; `shuttleO2Hours()`). Research **Tern air scrubbers** (3 points) makes it **10 hours**. **It is breathed by whoever is aboard:** the draw is proportional to the number aboard over 8, so a lone pilot lasts 8 times as long. **Air refills in 1 hour** (`O2_CHARGE_H`) in the bay or moored at a dock. Needs 20% air to launch. At 15% air on the way out she turns back (`shuttle-o2-low`); at 0% she is **adrift and out of air** (`shuttle-o2-out`; no crew are lost yet). The range shown and the send-to-dock check use the lower of the battery and the air. Saved as `o2`.
- **Seats:** 2 in the cockpit, **6 passengers** (`SHUTTLE_COCKPIT`, `SHUTTLE_SEATS`). **Usually 2 pilots; 1 pilot and a radio link is enough.** With one pilot the shuttle needs a link (within 20 nm of the ship, or of a dock's shore station, `SHUTTLE_LINK_NM`) or it turns back ("lost her radio link"). No pilot, no launch. Pilots and passengers are set on the bay page (taps cycle 0 to max) while she is in the bay. The pilots are abstract (crew stations are not emptied).
- **Equipment:** navigation and radio. **It does not scan.**
- **Airlocks cycle in 10 seconds** (`AIRLOCK_S`): at a dock before the exchange, when the team boards, and when she is taken aboard (recovery is 1 minute plus 10 s).
- **Trips:** *Send to <dock>* = out, airlock, home (crew and supplies). With passengers aboard, *Drop team at <dock>* leaves the team ashore and the shuttle **moored, charging and taking on air** at the dock until *Pick up the team* (airlock, team aboard, home). Routes go round land like WSKRs. Destinations are docks only for now.
- Modes: `follow` (alongside, rides with the ship), `going`, `exchange`, `moored`, `returning`. Events with crew speech: `bay-launched`, `shuttle-arrived`, `shuttle-exchanged`, `shuttle-landed`, `shuttle-alongside`, `shuttle-aboard`, `shuttle-low`, `shuttle-o2-low`, `shuttle-nolink`, `shuttle-dead`, `shuttle-o2-out`. Saved: `navigation.shuttle`.
- **Known test failures** (before and after the 2026-11-22 work): `tools/tern_test.py` (the out-of-range queue and hourly check-in checks, plus a tap timeout).
- **Not built:** the shuttle on the craft views; destinations other than docks; away-team missions that use `pax` and `ashore` (mission triggers for "team ashore"); named away-team members; supplies as items; crew orders by communicator ("Launch the shuttle"); a dry dock; consequences for the people aboard when the air runs out.

## "Ask about…" topics (communicator phase 3, built 2026-11-19)
- Each crew member's menu has **Ask about …** (after Make small talk) when they have a topic open. Topics are in `TOPICS` in `topics.js`: `id`, `label`, `when`, `lines`. `when` can be `{ talk: n }` (the Captain has said hello that many times, `progress.talk.<id>.n`), `{ flag }` (a lesson flag in `progress.flags`) or `{ after: topic }`. Saved: `progress.topics.<id> = { told: [], asked: {} }`. Since 2026-11-22 topics sit under a **Background** folder and **How do I…** guides sit beside them (`UI_NOTES.md`).
- A topic is **told once** (all its lines as one reply), then shown with a tick; asking again gets "I have told you what I can about that". The reply goes through the normal communicator reply and transcript.
- **`declines: true`** topics are never told: the person puts the Captain off, differently each time, and the last line repeats. **ARIA's "Captain Voss"** (opens after the first hello) is the first: "I will not lie to you about this, I will only decline", and she asks to be told if the Captain finds out. Dr. Patel and Lt. Kim have a Voss topic that tells what they know (they do not know why).
