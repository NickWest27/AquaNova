# Aqua Nova: ship and propulsion

Written 2026-11-20. The owner's brief: the Aqua Nova is a **sister ship of the SeaQuest DSV, a slightly later model**, and he is fairly sure it has **fusion reactors**.

## What the SeaQuest DSV 4600 had (web research, fan wikis; sources disagree in places)
- **Power: twin fusion reactors**, fuelled by **tritium taken from sea water**. Confirmed in the sources found, so the owner's guess was right.
- **Drive:** one source calls it **"Aqua Return Jets"** (water drawn in, heated by the reactors, thrown out aft), another **"Hydromagnetic Drive"** (magnetohydrodynamic: no moving parts). It is the same idea told two ways.
- **Top speed:** about **160 knots**. Length about 307 m. Crew about 88 military and 124 science. Crush depth over 9 km.
- **Hull:** a "bio-skin" outer layer. Craft: Stinger shuttles, a Sea Crab, and WSKR-type remote vehicles.
- Fiction dates: original 2014, 4600-II 2021.
- Sources: everybodywiki.com/SeaQuest_DSV_4600, a Wikipedia mirror, wiki.moltenaether.com/db/SeaQuest_Class_Submarine.

## What the game does today
- Ryan Chen's bio already says he was on the team that **commissioned the fusion plant**. So the Aqua Nova is already fusion powered in the story.
- The game's battery and oxygen model is a game abstraction: battery is the **ship's reserve and hotel load**, not the reactor. Nothing needs to change in the sim.

## Proposed canon (owner to confirm or change)
- The Aqua Nova has **twin fusion reactors fuelled by tritium extracted from sea water** (so it never refuels, but the plant needs time and care).
- **Drive name: the owner's call.** Options: *Aqua Return Jets* (plain and nautical), *Hydromagnetic Drive* (technical, no moving parts), or a Nova-specific name for a later model (for example a quieter second-generation drive). Suggest a quieter, more efficient drive than the 4600, which fits a research and survey ship with an Area of Operations limit.
- Top speed in the game is lower than 160 kt for balance. The story reason can be a **charter limit**, not a physical one, in line with the Area of Operations idea.
- Ryan Chen is the voice for all of it (an ARIA or Ryan topic, "the reactors", is not built yet).
