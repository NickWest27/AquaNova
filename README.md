# AquaNova

Welcome aboard the Aqua Nova, humanity’s most advanced deep-sea exploration vessel. As her Captain, you stand at the threshold of Earth’s last great frontier—not among distant stars, but in the alien darkness beneath our own waves.
For generations, we’ve gazed upward, searching the cosmos for extraterrestrial life and answers to our origins. Yet the truth lay closer than we imagined: the ocean depths harbor ecosystems more bizarre and wondrous than any science fiction—bioluminescent hunters prowling eternal night, impossible creatures thriving in crushing pressures, and geological mysteries that rewrite our understanding of the planet itself.
Your mission is one of discovery and diplomacy. The Aqua Nova is equipped with cutting-edge research laboratories, remotely operated vehicles, and defensive systems for encounters with the unexpected. You’ll lead a diverse crew of scientists, engineers, and specialists into uncharted waters where each dive reveals creatures that seem more alien than anything Hollywood could imagine.
Chart unexplored trenches. Document species unknown to science. Navigate international waters and the politics of ocean territories. Respond to distress calls from underwater installations. And always remember: in the depths, you’re never quite sure what’s looking back at you from the darkness.
The stars can wait. The abyss will not.
